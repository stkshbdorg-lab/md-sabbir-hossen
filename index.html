About

MD Sabbir Hossen (born 10 November 2005) is a researcher and writer from Satkhira, Bangladesh. He researches society, history, science and religion — his method is to question, to examine closely, and to face honest counterarguments.

He is a devoted literature enthusiast and a thirsty seeker of knowledge; the hunger to know is the lifeblood of everything he writes. He writes long, research-driven documentary-style essays, philosophical works, and detective novels.

Writing

Facebook Documentary — ফেসবুক ডকুমেন্টারি

A long-form, evidence-driven Bengali essay series in a documentary style — history, politics, society and civilisation examined in depth, with every claim paired with honest counterpoints.

The Great Reset — দ্য গ্রেট রিসেট

A research series in ten chapters on the crisis of modern civilisation, technology, and the future of humanity.

The Aniket–Rahat Detective Series — অনিকেত–রাহাত গোয়েন্দা সিরিজ

A psychology-grounded Bengali detective novel series featuring detective Aniket Chowdhury and narrator Rahat Hasan: Srabonratrer Jolchhap (শ্রাবণরাত্রির জলছাপ), Ojuhāt Songrahok (অজুহাত সংগ্রাহক, prequel), Dwitiyo Mrityu (দ্বিতীয় মৃত্যু, Case #1), Shesh Chithi (শেষ চিঠি, Case #2), and Guptodhon (গুপ্তধন, Case #3).

Philosophy

“I do not write for likes or comments, but to preserve my self. The flesh-and-blood person will die one day — but through writing, I can be re-imagined and re-thought for centuries to come.”
