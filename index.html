About

Profile

MD Sabbir Hossen is a researcher and writer from Satkhira, Bangladesh. He researches society, history, science and religion — to question, to examine closely, and to face honest counter-arguments is his method. He is a devoted lover of literature and a knowledge-thirsty reader.

He writes long, research-based documentary-style essays, philosophical pieces, and psychology-based detective novels. His writings are published on Facebook — in a daily rhythm, as serialized series.
